About the maps

Schematic, not to scale. Built from public airside guides: T3 domestic gates 117–120 north of the glass partition with bus gates 131–134 at ground level; international gates south along the moving walkway and the south-eastern finger; lounges on Level 4. T1 mid-section with West arm (Gates 9, 15 and hub 10–14, 17) and East arm (Gates 2, 7 and hub 3–6); Gates 1 and 16 down stairs. International gate order in T3 and shop positions are placeholders until MIAA plans arrive.
